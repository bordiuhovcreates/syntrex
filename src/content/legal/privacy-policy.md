Syntrex («we», «us» or «our») respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website (the «Website»), request a freight quote or subscribe to our newsletter, how we use it and what rights you have. We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 («GDPR») and the applicable laws of the Netherlands.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Syntrex, 145 Harbor Avenue, Rotterdam 3011, Netherlands. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at [hello@syntrex.com](mailto:hello@syntrex.com) or +31 10 587 24 90.

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal data** — any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, such as a name, email address or phone number.
- **Processing** — any operation performed on personal data, such as collecting, storing, using or deleting it.
- **Processor** — a third party that processes personal data on our behalf, for example an email or hosting provider.

## 3. Data we collect

We only collect the data needed to respond to you and provide our services:

- **Quote request form:** first name, last name (optional), email address, phone number, company name (optional) and the content of your message.
- **Newsletter:** your email address.
- **Technical data:** when you open the Website, your browser automatically shares technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit with our hosting and font providers. We do not use this data to identify you.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on the Website.

## 4. Why we use your data

- To answer your request and prepare a freight quote — on the basis of steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- To send our newsletter — on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To keep the Website secure and working properly — on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

## 5. Who receives your data

Your data is only shared with service providers that help us run the Website and handle requests:

- **Google** (Google Workspace and Google Fonts) — receives form submissions by email, stores them in a secure spreadsheet and delivers the fonts used on the Website.
- **GitHub** (GitHub Pages) — hosts the Website.

These providers act on our instructions and are bound by data processing agreements. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area; in that case, transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Quote requests are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless a contract follows — then we keep the data for as long as required by law, for example for tax and accounting purposes.
- Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email [hello@syntrex.com](mailto:hello@syntrex.com). We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## 8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to the systems that store form submissions.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
